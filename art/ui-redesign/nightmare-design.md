# Nightmare UI direction

The user replaced the initial ivory direction with a darker, more surreal nightmare theme. `nightmare-mockups.png` is the revised mockup sheet created before implementing that direction. Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.

The implementation preserves the existing arena and characters, uses Newsreader headings and DM Sans controls, near-black backgrounds, bone text, and muted red accents. The illustrated menu background is generated artwork; all text, icons, controls, and meters are live Godot UI. Gameplay mechanics remain unchanged.

## Mockup prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Edit the attached four-screen Project R.A.T. UI concept board. Keep the four-screen layout and good functional hierarchy, but completely replace the bright friendly visual atmosphere. The user wants a MUCH DARKER, surreal, unnerving, chaotic nightmare aesthetic matching their game's watching trees, faceless observers, impossible eyes, many-eyed animals and distorted garden. Sophisticated independent horror game, psychological nightmare, scratchy etching, ink, charred paper, torn photographs. Near-black #101013 and muted blood red #963c3b, dusty bone white #ded8cc for readable text. Not cute, not cozy, not clean white. Let strange artwork create menace while controls stay clear and usable.
Title screen top-left: bone serif typography 'Project R.A.T.' with slightly irregular printing, left aligned. Behind it, very dark quiet space. Right half has a haunting surreal horror charcoal etching: an oversized staring eye suspended among tangled roots, a lanky faceless watcher, red threads, many small hidden eyes, distorted crooked garden fences and threatening animal silhouettes. A tiny stylized rat with pink ears and red scarf may appear low in the composition dwarfed by the watcher; NOT a big friendly photorealistic Pixar mascot. Controls 'Start run' on a muted red rectangular button with clean bone sans text, secondary 'Settings' as simple bone text. Remove cute slogans. Bottom keyboard hints stay small and legible. No random lorem ipsum symbols, no skull/gothic cliches, no conventional moon crescent, no glossy neon.
Settings top-right: deep charcoal panel, elegant bone serif title 'Settings'. Fine smoky dividers, two columns 'Sound & comfort' and 'Keyboard'. ONLY two sliders 'Volume' and 'Camera shake'. Four switches 'Aim assist', 'Easy mode', 'Large text', 'Hints'. Keyboard label/key pairs. Muted red active controls, dusty white handles. Background has very faint unnerving scratch marks and hidden eyes at the edges, never across text. Text-only 'Done'.
Upgrade bottom-left: title 'Choose an upgrade', small 'Wave clear', subtitle 'Your choice lasts for the rest of this run.' Three dark translucent choices with a fine red focus edge on the first, irregular unsettling small line drawings for Bounce (seed bouncing like an eye), Dash bomb (thorn burst), Orbit (three eyes in orbit). Large readable titles and honest descriptions, bottom 'Choose [1]' etc. Sparse controls against uncanny edge art.
Gameplay bottom-right: retain a top-down arena composition but make it a surreal pitch-dark nightmare garden with skewed checkered floor, watching trees and giant eye motifs painted onto ground, strange animal enemies. Preserve compact HUD organization and clear arena center. Thin dusty bone health bar, muted red dash bar, top-left wave/encounter, top-right score, bottom-right pause/autofire. No clutter, no unreadable lettering, no large dashboard cards.
Deliver a cohesive 2x2 board, four full 16:9 game screens, very high-fidelity professionally designed horror UI. It should feel unnerving and unhinged in its art, with crisp restrained usable UI.

## Background asset prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create a production background art asset for the Project R.A.T. game title screen, based on the TOP LEFT nightmare illustration in the preceding dark four-screen UI mockup. This output is ONE FULL 16:9 landscape illustration, not a UI board. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, NO buttons, NO frames.
A profoundly unsettling surreal garden in scratchy woodcut/charcoal etched illustration: enormous organic staring eye tangled in trees near the upper right-center, very tall thin faceless watcher rooted into the ground at the right, smaller eyes inside branches, a few many-eyed animal silhouettes at the far right bottom, crooked iron fence and broken stone path in the lower right. Sparse dark red threads weave through the roots. Distorted anatomy and impossible perspective, blackened paper texture, muted bone scratches and dried blood red. Sophisticated psychological horror album art, not conventional fantasy, no moon, no neon, no shiny 3D.
CRITICAL COMPOSITION: The LEFT 43 percent must be very dark #101013 almost empty blackened paper, suitable for clear menu typography and controls. Only a few faint strands near the far left edge. All detailed artwork lives in the RIGHT 57 percent and bottom-right. Lower-left quiet too. Strong right-side composition like a looming nightmare, giant watchful eye upper-right-center facing viewer. No friendly mascot or foreground rat; the game's existing character will be composited by its renderer. Keep details visible, not crushed to black, on right. 16:9, high quality, finished texture, crisp fine linework.
