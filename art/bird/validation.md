# Reference bird validation

- Built in Blender 5.2.1 LTS from the supplied bird drawing, preserved in `art/references/bird-design.png` and packed into the editable source. The same geometry is installed in the cast library and shared recipe.
- Visually reviewed the studio render, all eight directions, and production arena capture. Preserved the hollow orange body/head outline, black eye dot, yellow beak, asymmetric white feather wings with golden eyes, and human feet. Refined feather coverage, round irises, foot lighting and toe anatomy. Increased opposing wing depth after checking head-on views.
- Final GLB: 29,677 triangles, 21,493 source vertices, one mesh, four materials, no texture images, 955,952 bytes. The paint exports unlit; feather, skin and eye surfaces stay shaded. Both GLB copies match, vertex colors survive import, and Godot generates LODs.
- Eight 192 x 192 RGBA sprites total 94,837 bytes, 59% smaller than the previous 230,126-byte bird set. All eight directions are distinct and nonempty, keep transparent margins, and have visible wing width. The orange fill remains exact, with transparent space inside the body outline. Sources, references and GLBs are excluded from the web package.
- Passed `bird_model_test`, `model_art_test`, `snake_model_test`, `owl_model_test`, `fox_model_test`, `cat_model_test`, `top_down_test`, `smoke_test`, `fun_systems_test`, `playability_test`, `keyboard_aim_test`, and `balance_test` with Godot 4.7.2. Rechecked bird integration and sprite framing after final geometry adjustments.
- Verified the opening-wave bird introduction, 16-unit collision radius, one-seed health, weaving pursuit and an actual seed projectile kill. The production renderer preserves authored colors and provides room for the wings, feet and health bar.
- Created the release Web export and checked browser loading and Play interaction through the opening-wave upgrade screen, with no console errors. The native production capture stages real birds at several facings; combat behavior is covered by the regression test.
- The model is static, without an armature or animation clips. Movement, bounce, squash and combat effects remain procedural. Wing backs and depth are inferred from the single supplied reference.
