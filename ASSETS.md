The visuals are used as downloaded. Core gameplay silhouettes remain procedurally drawn so the top-down combat readability stays consistent.

Project R.A.T.'s warm cartoon character art, backyard arena, particles, and UI are generated by the project's GDScript. This keeps every gameplay silhouette consistent at the top-down camera angle and avoids mixing static face tiles or side-view sprites into the moving arena. The sound effects below are redistributed under Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0).
