Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production concept sheet for Project R.A.T., a silly backyard picnic survival game, in the chunky expressive low-poly late Nintendo 64 cartoon style of Conker's Bad Fur Day. Original characters, sculptable simple silhouettes, big eyes, crooked teeth, goofy attitudes, warm saturated colors. Cream background, neat labeled grid, full body three-quarter views. Ten cast designs: RAT (gray bucktoothed hero, red neckerchief, oversized pink ears, seed blaster); BIRD (blue furious tiny bird, ridiculous yellow quiff); CAT (purple fat smug cat, single fang); OWL (brown grumpy owl with enormous cream eye disks); SNAKE (green coiled doofus with forked tongue); RACCOON (gray masked bin bandit with trash lid); FOX (orange lanky smug ambusher with giant white-tipped tail); ALPHA CAT (magenta royal cat with crooked gold crown); JUNKYARD DOG (tan bulldog, spiked red collar, floppy tongue); BARN OWL (cream giant owl with graduation cap). Bottom prop strip: cheese wedge with holes, red chili, three peas in pod, acorn, sugar cube, tin lid, needle tooth, sunflower seed, feather, venom blob, bone, sonic ring, crumb bomb with fuse, fizzy soda can. Title PROJECT R.A.T. / PICNIC PUBLIC ENEMIES. These are mockups for Blender production; emphasize clear shapes and amusing expressions.
